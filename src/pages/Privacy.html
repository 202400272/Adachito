Information We Collect

Most of AdashimaVerse can be browsed without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

We may receive information when you voluntarily use the site's feedback feature.

Feedback Submissions

When you submit feedback, the following information may be sent to our feedback service:

  • Feedback type, title, and description
  • Your email address, if you choose to provide one
  • The page URL and page title
  • Website version
  • Browser and operating-system information
  • Screen resolution and viewport dimensions
  • Language and timezone
  • Submission timestamp

We use this information to review feedback, investigate technical problems, improve the website, and respond when you voluntarily provide contact information.

Please do not submit passwords, payment information, or other highly sensitive information through the feedback form.

Local Storage

AdashimaVerse uses your browser's local storage to remember certain preferences and functionality, such as language, appearance, reader settings, viewing preferences, and playback progress.

An unfinished feedback form may also be temporarily saved in your browser so an accidental page close does not immediately erase your draft. These locally stored values are not sent to AdashimaVerse merely because they exist in your browser.

You can clear local storage through your browser's site-data or privacy settings. Doing so may reset saved preferences or progress.

Cookies and Advertising

AdashimaVerse does not currently use cookies for advertising or cross-site behavioral tracking. The website may use browser storage and third-party services required for particular functionality.

Third-Party Services

The feedback system uses EmailJS to transmit feedback submissions. EmailJS may process request metadata according to its own policies.

AdashimaVerse may also load or link to third-party websites, media, fonts, and services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, which AdashimaVerse does not control.

Retention and Sharing

Feedback may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to review submissions, investigate problems, maintain website records, or respond to the person who submitted them. Third-party service retention may also apply.

AdashimaVerse does not sell or rent visitor information. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users, or the rights and safety of others.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection and to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply to you, you may have rights concerning personal information processed through AdashimaVerse. These may include the right to be informed, access your information, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, request portability, or lodge a complaint with an appropriate data-protection authority.

If you have submitted feedback and have a privacy request concerning that submission, contact AdashimaVerse using the contact method provided on the website. We may need sufficient information to verify that a request relates to you.

Children's Privacy

AdashimaVerse is a general-audience fan website and does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from visitors. The feedback form does not require an account or an email address.

External Links

AdashimaVerse contains links to external websites and services. Once you leave AdashimaVerse, the destination site's privacy practices apply.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's functionality, services, or data-processing practices change. The date above indicates when it was most recently revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information submitted through AdashimaVerse, please use the contact method provided on the website.

AdashimaVerse is an independently operated, international, non-profit fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators, publishers, licensors, or other rights holders of Adachi to Shimamura.
