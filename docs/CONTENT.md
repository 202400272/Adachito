# Content, Sources & Rights

## Archive content

AdaShimaverse is an unofficial fan archive. Its purpose is to organize information and provide access to material related to the series and its adaptations.

Catalogue entries should clearly distinguish:

- original source material;
- manga and other adaptations;
- official publication information;
- fan-translated reading resources;
- supplementary material.

## Translations

The archive does not represent fan translations as official translations. Where the official release is not available in a user's language, the archive may link to or provide access to fan-translated works according to the project's existing content policy.

Do not label fan translations as publisher-authorized or official.

## Accuracy

Use reliable publication information and preserve the project's established naming and numbering conventions. If a correction is made, update the underlying JSON data rather than patching a single rendered page.

When adding series news or development updates, keep the two categories distinct:

- **Series news** concerns developments in the source series, adaptations, releases, or related official information.
- **Dev updates** concern AdaShimaverse itself, such as archive changes, site improvements, fixes, or new features.

## Copyright

The archive does not claim ownership of third-party characters, books, manga, illustrations, music, or other copyrighted material belonging to their respective rights holders.

Only include or link material in accordance with the project's rights and content policy. Do not add content merely because it can be found online.

## DMCA requests

The Help Center contains the public DMCA and copyright section. A rights holder or authorized representative who believes material hosted by AdaShimaverse infringes their rights can use that section to submit a removal request.

A contributor must not dismiss a copyright complaint on their own. Preserve the relevant information, avoid modifying evidence unnecessarily, and escalate the request through the project's designated contact process.

## Contributor rule

When uncertain whether material can be added, stop and ask for clarification rather than assuming that fan availability, search-engine visibility, or non-commercial use automatically makes redistribution permissible.
